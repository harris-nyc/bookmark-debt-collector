# Privacy

- **Your raw data stays local.** Pulls, the archive, overrides, state and all personal outputs live in `private/`,
  which is gitignored. Nothing in this repo uploads anything anywhere.
- **Read-only on X.** The skills forbid posting, liking, following, bookmarking, unbookmarking, deleting and DMs. When
  the right move is "unbookmark" or "DM them", it goes on *your* list.
- **Shareable outputs hide private topics.** Some bookmarks are nobody's business (dating, health, politics, venting).
  `src/bdc/privacy.py` flags a bookmark when any of these hold:
  - **keyword**: its text, the text of the post it quotes or replies to, or a link title hits the keyword list
    (extend it with `--private-keywords "a,b,c"`),
  - **sensitive theme**: it sits in a theme that's hidden from the card (below),
  - **same conversation**: it quotes a flagged bookmark, or it quotes the same *deleted or hidden* post as a flagged
    bookmark. A deleted quote is context we can't read, so a harmless-looking reply to it inherits its sibling's flag.

  Flagged posts:
  - still count toward totals (the card's numbers are honest),
  - never appear as text on the card (authors, oldest bookmark, skills are chosen from non-flagged posts),
  - are hidden from `actions.public.md` (the full `actions.md` keeps everything, for you),
  - are left out of `freshness.md` (counts stay; `freshness.json` has the full detail),
  - are never picked for the weekly resurface.

  **Themes** are kept off the card (and their posts treated as private) when the name starts with `_`, when they're
  listed under `"_hide"` in `theme_names.json` (or `--hide-themes`), when the name or top terms look personal (health,
  dating, looks, fitness, ...), or when at least a third of their posts are flagged (`--sensitive-theme-share`, 0 turns
  it off). Every flag and its reason is written to `privacy.json` in the output folder.
- **The filter is blunt.** It errs towards hiding, and it still misses things. Check the card, `actions.public.md` and
  `freshness.md` before you share them.
- **Third parties seen by the freshness check**: GitHub, arXiv and the linked sites get a plain `GET` from your machine
  (or the bot's computer). No cookies, no X credentials.
- **Example data is synthetic.** `examples/sample_bookmarks.json` is generated by `examples/make_synthetic.py`; every
  account, post and metric in it is invented.
